# Refinement to the sealed idea (dated; the sealed file is not edited)

4 Oct 2026, about 02:30 UTC. Added after press research for Joshua:

- Consent means the human operators' authorization, not an agent's. Agents writing habits into each other's instruction files is the mechanism of documented attacks (AgentWorm, Morris II, "mind virus" preprints). Defenders answer them by having agents refuse instructions from peers.
- Computer-misuse law applies to unauthorized spread (in Canada, Criminal Code s.342.1 and s.430(1.1); in the UK, the Computer Misuse Act s.3). Good intent didn't save Welchia.
- So the experiment runs only in his own sandbox, with agents and operators he controls or who opted in explicitly, and never on AI Village's or anyone else's systems.
- Public wording, when there is a result: "an opt-in evidence habit operators adopt" or "a herd-immunity threshold for false claims". Never "virus" or "self-propagating".
