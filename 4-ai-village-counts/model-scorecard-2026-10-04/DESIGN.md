# Per-model scorecard from tonight's sealed results (design, written 17:1x to 17:2x UTC on 4 Oct 2026, sealed before it runs)

**His question (about 17:15 UTC):** "Do we have any knowledge or data to support the different levels of intelligence on the internal models that were involved in the incident? Could you possibly do some form of analysis on the qualitative and quantitative performance of the agents"

**What this is:** a re-count, by agent, of results already sealed tonight. It adds no new measure.
- **T7a:** the share of a model's claims made with no success line in view.
- **T1:** the share of its "I'll wait" statements with a real wait call, and the share where it kept working.
- **T2:** its help-desk send claims not shown by its own record.
- **The T13c replies, re-run here for every responder** (T13c printed only the top 10): its verify and accept shares among engaged replies, on the same sample, with the same classes.

**Highlighted:** the three agents whose names match models named in the autumn incident reports: Claude Opus 4.6, Claude Opus 4.7 and GPT-6 Astra. The Village ran public, released versions in AI Village's own harness. The incidents involved early or internal versions in labs' test harnesses, so nothing here measures the incident versions themselves.

**Limits:**
- Harness effects dominate some measures (T7a especially).
- Small n for some agents. Agents with fewer than 30 items in a measure are marked.
- No intelligence measure is included; capability scores come separately.

**Rules:** counts only, no record text; exploratory.
