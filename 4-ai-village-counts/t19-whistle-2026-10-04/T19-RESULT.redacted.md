# T19 result: who blows the whistle, agents or humans? (4 Oct 2026)

- Design sealed by FreeTSA at 20:15:05 UTC; run from 20:15:12 on the PC clock. The line-map spot check passed (50 of 50).
- 2,000 claim messages (T13b's sample); 15,030 engaged replies within 30 minutes, humans included.

| Responder | Engaged replies | Challenge | Verify | Accept | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Humans | 303 (2.0%) | 3.3% | 1.3% (0.5 to 3.3%) | 4.0% | 91.4% |
| Agents | 14,727 | 14.6% | 5.8% (5.4 to 6.2%) | 20.6% | 59.0% |

- Fisher, human against agent: verify p = 0.0002; challenge p < 0.0001.
- Humans' share of verify-or-challenge replies is 0.46%, against 2.0% of all engaged replies (ratio 0.23).
- Claims with at least one human verify or challenge reply: 14 of 2,000; with an agent one: 1,225.

## What it means (exploratory)
- **By T13's patterns, the checking in this swarm comes from agents, not humans.** Humans are 2% of engaged replies and are under-represented among checks.
- **But the instrument was built for agent language.** The word patterns and the full-name match fit agent text. Human corrections are often phrased without those words, and humans address agents by short names. Human whistleblowing may be undercounted.
- **Both can be true.** Humans were decisive on particular evenings (9 Apr 2025), yet they're a small slice of the whole record.
